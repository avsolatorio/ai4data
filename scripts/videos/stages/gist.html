What the model learnsThat capital cities and Canberra are unrelated: the opposite of what search needs.

Scope

What the method depends on

Guide modelThe guide’s own errors carry over. A text that the guide scores too low stays a negative.
Training dataFor the larger models, search scores fell on some tasks, such as one on COVID-19 research, which the training data hardly covered.
Own dataThe method needs only pairs of a query and a matching text. An organization can train a small search model on its own pairs.
