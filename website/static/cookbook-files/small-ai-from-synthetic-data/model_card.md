# World Bank topic classifier (example)

This card describes the classifier trained in the running example of the
*Practical Guide to Small AI from Synthetic Data*. It is filled in as an
example; an organization replaces each value with its own.

## Model

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Task | Suggest up to five topics from the World Bank's topic list (86 labels) for an English document on economic and social development |
| Base model | microsoft/deberta-v3-small, 142 million parameters, MIT License |
| Output | One probability per label for each sentence; a document's score per label is the mean over its sentences |
| Decision rule | Labels with a document score of at least 0.02, at most five, highest first (chosen on 1,492 calibration abstracts) |
| Size | 568 megabytes in full precision |
| Speed | 0.17 seconds per abstract on four CPU cores; 0.007 seconds on one NVIDIA H100 GPU |

## Intended use

Suggestions to the curators of a document collection, who accept or
change them. The model is not intended for counting documents by topic
in published statistics without a separate evaluation of that use.

## Training data

20,054 sentences generated by gpt-oss-120b (Apache License 2.0) with
prompt version v2-allow-names, described in their dataset card. The
sentences were split by generation request: 16,044 for training, 2,006
for development, and 2,004 for test.

## Training

Fine-tuned for four passes with `train_classifier.py`: batch size 32,
learning rate 0.00005, weight decay 0.01, full precision, seed 42.
Tested with torch 2.9.1 and transformers 4.57.3.

## Evaluation

| Test set | Micro F1 | Macro F1 |
|---|---|---|
| 2,004 generated test sentences (upper bound), sentence threshold 0.25 | 0.758 | 0.765 |
| 300 abstracts of World Bank Policy Research Working Papers, 2016 to 2020, with labels from the catalogue's subjects through a crosswalk (44 labels measured) | 0.460 | 0.380 |
| The same 300 abstracts, labelled by gpt-oss-120b from the label names, for comparison | 0.511 | 0.421 |

A second training run with another seed scored 0.467 in micro F1 on the
300 abstracts.

## Known errors

- Labels assigned far more often than the reference uses them: Competition Policy (22 abstracts against 2), Debt (18 against 3), and Higher Education (30 against 5) on the 300 abstracts.
- Lowest per-label F1 on the 300 abstracts: Water Resources Management 0.10 (partly a crosswalk effect), Urban Development 0.18, Digital Development 0.27, and Governance 0.27.
- 42 of the 86 labels do not occur in the reference labels of the 300 abstracts, because the catalogue's subjects do not map to them, so their quality on real documents is unknown.
- The model reads one sentence at a time and does not use the title or the position of a sentence.
