## Interpretation of the result

- Chapters 1 and 4 come first: scope and safety are decisions, and they are
  cheap compared with their cost when skipped.
- Chapter 2 depends on the API and catalog described in the dissemination
  cookbook; a server over an undocumented API inherits every gap.
- Chapter 5 is the gate for publication: a server without an evaluation
  suite cannot be changed safely.
