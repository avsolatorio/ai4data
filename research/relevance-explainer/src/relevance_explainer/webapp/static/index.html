The model reads the search, the filters, the retriever's similarity score, a deterministic filter check and the item's metadata, then writes a verdict line and two or three sentences in the query's language. Decoding is greedy with a 4-gram repeat block; the script constraint blocks characters of scripts that neither the requested language nor the input uses.

Training. Qwen3.8-27B wrote verdicts and narratives for searches over World Bank Data360 indicators, Microdata Library studies, Policy Research Working Papers and Humanitarian Data Exchange datasets. The student was pretrained on Wikipedia in 11 languages, fine-tuned on the task data and preference-tuned with DPO.

Reading the labels. About one test example in five has a contested label: the teacher, the teacher in thinking mode and Gemma 4 do not all agree, usually between partly relevant and irrelevant. Examples marked ⚑ are such cases. The offline judge scores are for the best model's output on the unedited input.
